# Context

## The problem

Mattan has a lot to remember. Two kinds of things already have a home:

| Kind | Tool |
|---|---|
| Dates and deadlines (schoolwork, events) | Calendar |
| Immediate, finishable tasks | To-do list |
| **Things to keep in mind**: ideas, notes, principles, ongoing concerns, things about people | **Nothing yet** |

The third kind has no due date and no "done" state. It slips away because nothing brings it back up, and there's no good place to look it up either.

## The idea

**Dashboard** is one place to drop those things, plus a weekly **physical** reminder. Each week the active items are printed on paper, so there's something real to look at and the items stay fresh in Mattan's mind.

## The weekly ritual

1. During the week, Mattan adds items from their phone or any device, whenever something comes up.
2. On Sunday morning (default), the sheet prints by itself on the home Canon printer.
3. An email arrives with the PDF attached. That's both the notification and a digital copy.
4. Mattan picks up the sheet and reads it over. Anything no longer relevant gets archived in the app.

## Principles

- **Low maintenance.** After setup it should run without attention. A missed week (laptop away from home) catches up on its own.
- **Unobtrusive.** Claude only groups similar items. It doesn't comment, summarize or reword. The sheet shows Mattan's own words.
- **Fast capture.** Adding an item should take only a few seconds on a phone.

## Out of scope

- Due dates, reminders, recurring tasks, checkboxes. That's what the calendar and to-do list are for.
- Notifications beyond the one weekly email.
- Sharing or multiple users. This is a single-person tool.
- AI features beyond grouping.

## What success looks like

- A sheet appears on the printer every week without Mattan touching anything.
- Adding an item from the phone is quick enough that Mattan actually does it.
- The sheet fits on one page, or two at most, and reads easily.
